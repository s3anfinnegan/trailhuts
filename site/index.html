Before you go

Many shelters on this map sit on Coillte, National Park, or private land. There is no general legal right of access in Ireland — a shelter being marked here does not guarantee a right to be on the land around it. Always check the access notes on each shelter, and respect any registration requirements (e.g. Wild Nephin National Park asks hikers to register before an overnight stay).

Shelters marked unverified have not been confirmed recently — treat their location and condition as provisional and have a backup plan. Conditions change: a shelter shown here may be damaged, removed, or harder to reach than expected.

Practice Leave No Trace: carry out what you carry in, don't light fires where not explicitly permitted, and leave the shelter better than you found it for the next hiker.
